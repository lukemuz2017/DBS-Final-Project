# Methods, user need, regulation: personal treasury app

## 1. Corporate treasury practices that translate
- **13-week cash flow forecast:** a rolling, weekly, direct-method (receipts minus disbursements) view. Each week the past week drops off and a new one is added. Maps to "next 3 months, week by week, paycheck to paycheck." https://trovata.io/guides/13-week-cash-forecasting/ ; https://stampli.com/resources/13-week-cash-flow-forecast/
- **Liquidity tiering:** operating cash, then a reserve buffer, then strategic or long-term money. Maps to checking, then an emergency fund in high-yield savings, then investments. (General treasury practice; I found no single source.)
- **Cash positioning and sweeps:** each day, check balances and sweep excess above a target into higher-yield accounts, or pull funds back to cover shortfalls. Maps to "move $X above the floor to savings."
- **Forecast vs. actual variance:** compare each week and fix the assumptions that keep missing. Ripple notes that most teams build a forecast but fewer track its accuracy. Eagle Rock CFO says 90%+ accuracy for weeks 1-4 and 80-85% for weeks 9-13, but cites no study. https://treasury.ripple.com/posts/cash-flow-forecasting-best-practices ; https://www.eaglerockcfo.com/blog/treasury-management/13-week-cash-forecast
- **Stress testing:** keep a base case, an upside, a downside and a stress scenario. Maps to "lose a gig client," "a $1,000 car repair," "rent goes up 10%." JPMorgan Chase Institute already frames household saving as a "monthly stress test." https://www.jpmorganchase.com/institute/research/household-income-spending/report-weathering-volatility-2-a-monthly-stress-test-to-guide-saving

## 2. Forecasting methods for personal transaction data
- **Recurring detection:** group by normalized merchant, amount (within a tolerance) and cadence (weekly, biweekly, monthly). Plaid flags streams with fewer than 3 occurrences as immature. https://plaid.com/blog/recurring-transactions/
- **Academic baseline:** Zhang et al. 2018 (low-wage workers) combines recurring detection from description heuristics, historical averages and regularized regression to predict balances. I did not get its accuracy numbers; check the paper. https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.05362
- **Residual spending:** use seasonal or time-series models (day-of-week and payday seasonality, ETS/ARIMA/Prophet) on the non-recurring spending left over. Give a range (quantiles), not a single number. Cash withdrawals and irregular income make the data noisier.
- **LLMs for categorization:** the published accuracy data is weak and comes mostly from vendors. Digits' study (17,792 SMB transactions) found no general-purpose LLM above about 73%, and an earlier version reported about 66%. Other projects say rules + ML + LLM ensembles beat an LLM alone (LLM alone about 92%). An SME study found LLMs underperform without context. https://digits.com/downloads/beyond-the-hype-evaluating-llms-vs-digits-agl.pdf ; https://transaction-ai.readthedocs.io/en/latest/BENCHMARKS/ ; https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.05425
- **Takeaway:** use deterministic rules and statistics for the forecast. Use the LLM for messy categorization, explanations and natural-language "what-if" scenarios.

## 3. User need
- **Fed SHED 2025 (released May 2026):** 63% of adults would cover a $400 emergency with cash or its equivalent, so about 37% would not. That is flat from 2024 and down from 68% in 2021. 59% had a major unexpected expense in the past year, and 73% are "doing okay or living comfortably." https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/other20260513a.htm ; https://www.federalreserve.gov/consumerscommunities/shed.htm
- **JPMorgan Chase Institute:** income and spending each swing about 30% month to month (2017). The median household's income volatility was about 36%, and it stayed constant from 2013 to 2018. 84% of people saw income change by more than 5% month to month. Income dips hit low-income families hardest. The sample is Chase customers only. https://www.jpmorganchase.com/institute/all-topics/household-financial-health/report-weathering-volatility ; https://www.jpmorganchase.com/content/dam/jpmc/jpmorgan-chase-and-co/institute/pdf/institute-insight-financial-stress-test.pdf
- **Gig work:** the Fed SHED (Oct 2024) found 20% of adults did gig work in the prior month, and about 21% of them treated it as their main job. BLS put alternative arrangements as a main job at 10.2% in 2023. https://www.federalreserve.gov/consumerscommunities/shed.htm

## 4. Regulation
- **Investment advice:** personalized advice about securities for compensation makes you an investment adviser under the Advisers Act s.202(a)(11). The "publisher" exclusion (Lowe v. SEC, 1985) covers only impersonal, general-circulation content. Personalized robo-advice falls outside it and requires RIA registration. SEC IM Guidance 2017-02 sets robo-adviser duties: disclosure, suitability questionnaires and compliance. https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-472/pdf/USREPORTS-472-181.pdf ; https://www.sec.gov/investment/im-guidance-2017-02.pdf
- **Safe zone:** cash and budgeting advice ("move $300 from checking to savings," "your balance dips below $0 on the 14th") is not securities advice. Keep stress tests to cash flows and generic scenarios, and don't name specific securities or allocations.
- **Moving money:** holding or moving other people's funds is money transmission, which needs state licenses in nearly every state plus FinCEN MSB registration. Most fintechs avoid it with a partner bank and FBO accounts, or as an agent of a licensee. Synapse's 2024 collapse (a $60-90M ledger shortfall, a CFPB suit and $46.2M in CFPB relief) shows the risk of FBO reconciliation. https://www.moderntreasury.com/journal/how-do-money-transmission-laws-work ; https://www.bankingdive.com/news/5-lessons-learned-from-synapses-collapse/731543/ ; https://www.pymnts.com/legal/2025/synapse-faces-cfpb-over-fund-reconciliation-gaps/
- **What a student or MVP can do safely:** read-only aggregation (Plaid sandbox), forecasts and cash-only recommendations the user carries out themselves. Avoid custody, auto-sweeps and security-specific advice. Add an "educational, not investment advice" disclaimer, and get counsel before automating transfers.
